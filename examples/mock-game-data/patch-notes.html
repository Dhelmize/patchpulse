Patch Notes v1.1.0

January 20, 2024

Character Balance Changes

Warrior

  • Increased base health from 100 to 110
  • Increased attack damage from 15 to 18
  • Shield Bash now stuns for 0.5 seconds longer

Mage

  • Decreased base health from 60 to 55
  • Fireball damage increased by 10%
  • Fixed bug where Fireball could hit invisible targets

Rogue

  • Backstab critical damage increased from 2x to 2.5x
  • Movement speed increased by 5%

New Content

  • Added new weapon: Legendary Axe (damage: 25, rarity: legendary)
  • Added new character class: Paladin

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed crash when entering dungeon level 10
  • Fixed visual glitch with weapon effects
  • Fixed memory leak in inventory system

Thanks for playing!
